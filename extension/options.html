GoFile browser credentials

Bunni asks only for GoFile access. When you hand off a GoFile download, it reads cookies matching that exact URL and sends them to the local desktop app for that request.

GoFile transfers use one connection for reliability because concurrent ranges can trigger its rate limit. Your general Download parts setting still applies to other sites.
